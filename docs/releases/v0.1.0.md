# v0.1.0: first production release (Sprint 1)

Candidate: **`v0.1.0-rc.1`** on `main` commit `6ac7ccb`, `main` CI green. Acceptance (http://127.0.0.1:8180) runs `360dd85`, which has the same app content: every merge after it touched only docs.

## What is in this release

- **Accounts:** register with an invite code, log in and out, rename, change password, delete (#23, #25); rate-limited forms show the wait (#39).
- **Pages:** every screen at its own address (#30); the redesigned landing page and the public `/demo` page (#42, #57).
- **Packs, on the server** (#28, #31, #38):
  - six tiers, with slot floors, pity at 300, Enchanted every 10th and Radiant 1 in 2 500;
  - the allowance of one pack every 10 minutes, up to 3;
  - opening at `/packs/open` (#57).
- **Collection** (#41): search, filters, sort, pages, the Series view, the card dialog.
- **Economy:**
  - the Karma ledger, Recycle for Karma and genre shop packs at `/shop` and `/shop/open` (#40, #51, #55);
  - the player market with listings, buying, the 5 % fee and the 7-day limits (#53, #60);
  - the owner commands `audit-ledger` and `audit-market`.

## Acceptance checklist (owner, in the browser at http://127.0.0.1:8180)

**Signed out:**

- [ ] I can see the landing page, open a demo pack, and reach `/demo`. Its cards have no glow, and the Join offer appears after the tenth pack.
- [ ] I can register with an invite code, and log in and out.

**Packs:**

- [ ] I can open a pack on `/packs`, see it on `/packs/open`, and go back.
- [ ] The countdown, the pity line and the odds dialog show.

**Collection:**

- [ ] I can search, filter, sort and page my collection.
- [ ] I can open a card's dialog and switch to the Series view.

**Karma and the shop:**

- [ ] I can recycle duplicates for Karma in the card dialog, and the header balance updates.
- [ ] I can buy a genre pack on `/shop` and see it on `/shop/open`.

**Market:**

- [ ] I can list a card on `/market` and cancel it. Buying needs a second account, because self-trades are refused.

**Account:**

- [ ] I can rename my account, change my password, and log out and back in.

Reply "approved v0.1.0" or "rejected v0.1.0: <reason>".
