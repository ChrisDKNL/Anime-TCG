Opening a pack, slot style

The ceremony's states S0 to S7, clickable. Hold the sleeve to tear it, tap or press Enter to turn each card, or use Skip and Reveal all. Every tell shows the card's real tier. Sound is off until you press the speaker in the stage's top bar.

        visual-identity.md §16 question 2; pack-opening.md §9. The tell and the spotlight play in both; only the turned card differs.

        What to try

        • The Showcase script turns one card of each tier from Uncommon to Mythic, so every tell and all three spotlights show in one run. No real pack deals it: a standard pack has one Rare-or-better card, in slot 5.
        • Press and release the sleeve quickly to see Keep holding; hold for 0.7 s to tear.
        • Tick Reduced motion: one tap tears, there is no burst, flip or spotlight, and Epic and up get the static banner.
        • Set the server answer to 12 s to see S0's waiting lines.
        • Enchanted, Radiant and Guaranteed Mythic change the sleeve's lit sockets and kind mark before the tear.
