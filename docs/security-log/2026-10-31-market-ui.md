- **Verdict:** PASS WITH FINDINGS (none blocking), reviewed at head 129e15a; finding 1 and the QC should-fix items fixed before merge in e6b0cb9, 1500c3d and bb5554a
- **Owner informed:** through the PR's sign-offs

## Change

Economy PR 4, web only (`docs/design/market-and-shop.md` §3.4–§3.8 and §8; `docs/design/collection-overview.md` §3.5 and §8; `economy.md` §11, §12; `packs-page.md` §3.3). No api, shared, dependency, CI or CSP change.

- **`/market`:**
  - An account page in the route list (guard to `/login` with the return path), linked in the header as `Market`.
  - Two views, Browse and My listings. The view, search, filters, sort and character filter live in the signed-in layout's state, never in the address or in storage.
- **Browse:**
  - `GET /api/market/listings` with `rarity`, `franchiseId`, `characterId`, `q` and `sort` as the request's query string.
  - The next page through `nextCursor`. A filter change asks for the first page again.
  - The listing dialog shows `Buy…`, or `Cancel listing` on the player's own listing.
- **Buy:**
  - One `POST /api/market/listings/{listingId}/buy` with `{ requestId }` only.
  - The receipt's card and `balance` are shown.
  - `market_limit` is held on the page: the buyer's limits for every listing, a seller's or pair's for that listing. The confirm is disabled for `retryAfterSeconds`, at most `MAX_WAIT_SECONDS`, falling back to `Retry-After`, else 60 s.
- **List on the market, in the card dialog:**
  - A `Price` field within the wallet's bounds for the entry's rarity.
  - A fee preview and net from the wallet's published rule.
  - One `POST /api/market/listings` with `{ requestId, characterId, rarity, price }`. The answer's `fee` replaces the preview.
- **My listings:**
  - Shows active, closed and purchased listings, and the window room as sent.
  - `Cancel listing` sends `{}` with no key, single-flight per listing, and its `Try again` resends it.
- **Keys (`useKeyedAction`):**
  - Kept only on no answer (a non-envelope answer counts as none) and on a 429.
  - Discarded on every other answer, on Cancel, on close and on unmount.
  - Single-flight plus the `detail > 1` and held-Enter guards.
  - Nothing closes either confirmation in flight.
- **Never:** no client storage beyond TanStack Router's `tsr-scroll-restoration-v1_3`, no query string or hash in the page address, no HTML sink.

## Findings

1. **Low:** no committed test proved that a `close` event in flight cannot close the market buy or listing confirmation (the #51 bug class). The behaviour was correct. Tests added before merge in e6b0cb9, 1500c3d and bb5554a.
2. **Info:** after no answer, Cancel, Close or leaving the page discards the key, so a new listing confirmation can list another copy. This is spec'd and bounded by the server; a purchase cannot repeat. Accepted.
3. **Info:** a `market_limit` hold is per tab and lost on reload; the server refuses again. Accepted.
4. **Info:** `economy.md` §14's stack e2e for the market is not in the suite; the two-account purchase is proven server-side by #53. Open item.
